Advisory Team Application

Equity Gaming · City of Manila Roleplay

Before answering the application form, please read the following carefully:

  • Please answer all questions honestly and completely.
  • Do not leave questions unanswered unless they are specifically marked as optional.
  • You may answer in English or Tagalog, whichever you are more comfortable with. What matters most is that your answers are clear and understandable.
  • Please use your own words and personal experiences when answering.
  • Avoid copying answers from other applicants, previous applications, templates, or online sources.
  • We encourage you to be genuine and honest rather than trying to give the “perfect” answer. There are no specific answers that will automatically guarantee acceptance.
  • Please do not simply answer what you think the staff wants to hear. We want to understand your personality, judgment, attitude, capabilities, and willingness to take responsibility.
  • For situational questions, explain what you would actually do and why. Short answers are acceptable as long as they properly explain your decision.
  • Please make sure that you understand the duties and responsibilities of an Administrator before submitting your application.
  • By submitting this form, you acknowledge that being an Administrator requires commitment, accountability, fairness, professionalism, and proper decision-making.
  • Duplicate, copied, or obviously rehearsed answers may affect the evaluation of your application. We value authenticity more than impressive wording.
  • Please review your answers carefully before submitting to make sure that everything is complete and accurate.
Your answers are only seen by the Head Advisor and Department of Public Relations.
