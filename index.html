What an Advisor does

Advisors are the first people players turn to. The role is about helping, explaining, and representing the server well.

Guide newcomers

Walk new players through the rules, the basics of roleplay, and how the city works, so their first days feel welcoming.

Answer questions

Be the go-to person in game and on Discord for questions about rules, systems, and how things work.

Support the staff

Pass concerns and issues to the right people, and help the Administration Team keep the community running smoothly.

Set the example

Follow the rules yourself, stay fair and professional, and show players what good roleplay and good conduct look like.

What we look for

There is no perfect answer that guarantees a spot. We want to see who you really are, so be honest and use your own words.

CommitmentYou can realistically give the time the role needs, and you tell us early when life gets in the way.
AccountabilityYou own your decisions and your mistakes, and you speak up when something is wrong, even if it involves a friend.
FairnessYou treat every player the same and stay calm when someone is rude to you.
ProfessionalismYou represent the server well, in game and out of it.

Questions

Quick answers before you apply.

Requirements

Can I answer in Tagalog?

Yes. You can answer in English or Tagalog, whichever you are more comfortable with.

Who can see my application?

Only the Head Advisor and the Director of Public Relations.

How will I know the result?

After you submit, you get a reference code. Use "Check application status" to see whether your application is under review, accepted, or not selected.

Can I apply again if I am not selected?

Yes. Only one application can be under review at a time, and you can apply once every 7 days.

I was staff before. Can I apply?

Yes. Choose "Reinstate" in the form.

What if I will be inactive?

File a leave of absence before you go, so everyone knows when to expect you back.

Before answering, please read carefully

  • Please answer all questions honestly and completely.
  • Do not leave questions unanswered unless they are marked as optional.
  • You may answer in English or Tagalog, whichever you are more comfortable with. What matters most is that your answers are clear and understandable.
  • Use your own words and personal experiences. Avoid copying answers from other applicants, previous applications, templates, or online sources.
  • Be genuine rather than trying to give the “perfect” answer. No specific answer will automatically guarantee acceptance, and we do not want to hear only what you think the staff wants to hear.
  • For situational questions, explain what you would actually do and why. Short answers are fine as long as they explain your decision.
  • Make sure you understand the duties and responsibilities of an Administrator before submitting.
  • By submitting, you acknowledge that being an Administrator requires commitment, accountability, fairness, professionalism, and proper decision-making.
  • Duplicate, copied, or obviously rehearsed answers may affect the evaluation of your application. We value authenticity more than impressive wording.
Your answers are only seen by the Head Advisor and the Director of Public Relations.
